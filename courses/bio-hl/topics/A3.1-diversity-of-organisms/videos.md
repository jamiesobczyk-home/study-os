# A3.1 Diversity of organisms — what to watch

Watch **one** main video, then close it and explain the topic without the screen. A second explanation can make the same ideas feel familiar without making them retrievable, and recognition is already the problem.

## Main

**Mister Lee Science — A3.1 Diversity of organisms**

- Channel: <https://www.youtube.com/@misterleescience> — verified
- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Mister+Lee+Science+A3.1+Diversity+of+organisms>

Alex Lee organizes the course by the current IB topic codes, so begin with the version built around A3.1.

**Watch for:**

- Why the biological species concept requires **fertile** offspring, not merely mating or hybrid offspring.
- How a karyogram differs from a karyotype and how homologous chromosomes are matched.
- Why a large genome doesn’t necessarily contain more genes or belong to a more complex organism.
- Which parts are HL only: asexual reproduction, horizontal gene transfer, dichotomous keys and environmental DNA.

**Pause at:** the first karyogram. Before the explanation continues, name the three features used to pair chromosomes, identify the sex-chromosome pair, and say whether you’re looking at a karyogram or a karyotype.

## If that didn’t land

**Amoeba Sisters — taxonomy and classification**

- Channel: <https://www.youtube.com/@AmoebaSisters> — verified
- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Amoeba+Sisters+taxonomy+classification+species>

Use this if the species concepts and scientific names are getting mixed together. Afterward, define the morphological and biological species concepts without replaying the video.

**Khan Academy — speciation and reproductive isolation**

- Channel: <https://www.youtube.com/@khanacademy> — verified
- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Khan+Academy+speciation+reproductive+isolation>

Use this if you know the definitions but can’t explain why species boundaries become unclear while populations are diverging.

## Worth it once, not for revision

Constructing a karyogram is a spatial sorting task, so a visual demonstration earns an exception to the one-video rule.

- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=karyogram+karyotype+chromosome+pairing+animation>

Pause before each chromosome is placed. Predict its partner from length, banding pattern and centromere position, then check the placement.
