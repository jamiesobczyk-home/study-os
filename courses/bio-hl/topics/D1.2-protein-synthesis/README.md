# D1.2 Protein synthesis

- **Theme:** D — Continuity and change
- **Level:** Molecules
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Protein synthesis copies a gene into RNA and translates its codons into an amino acid sequence, with RNA processing and protein modification expanding what cells can produce.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

D1.2 is the route from genotype to protein, so it keeps returning in mutations, inheritance and gene expression. The hard part is holding several complementary sequences and directions in your head while molecules move between roles. Draw the strands with 5′ and 3′ ends, then explain each arrow aloud instead of rereading the finished diagram.

## In your study guide

Allott, _Biology Study Guide_ (2023), **section D1.2, pp. 43–49**. Read it *after*
the capture sheet, never before. It's condensed enough to make a fast check of
what you left out, and a slow way to meet the topic for the first time.
