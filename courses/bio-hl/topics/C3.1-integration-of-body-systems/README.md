# C3.1 Integration of body systems

- **Theme:** C — Interaction and interdependence
- **Level:** Organisms
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Nerves, hormones and transported materials let specialized organs share information and coordinate whole-body responses, while plant hormones coordinate growth and development.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

C3.1 is difficult because every explanation is a pathway: a receptor detects something, information reaches an integration center, and an effector changes its activity. Naming the parts won’t usually finish the answer. Practice drawing arrows between them, then say what crosses each arrow. The same approach works for reflexes, heart and ventilation feedback, phototropism and ethylene feedback.

## In your study guide

Allott, _Biology Study Guide_ (2023), **section C3.1, pp. 134–143**. Read it *after*
the capture sheet, never before. It’s condensed enough to make a fast check of
what you left out, and a slow way to meet the topic for the first time.
