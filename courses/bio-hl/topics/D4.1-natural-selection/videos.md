# D4.1 Natural selection — what to watch

Watch **one** main video, then close it and produce the mechanism from memory. A second explanation can feel useful because you recognize the same sequence again, but recognition is the problem this pack is trying to expose.

## Main

**Mister Lee Science — D4.1 Natural selection**

- Channel: <https://www.youtube.com/@misterleescience> — verified
- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Mister+Lee+Science+D4.1+Natural+Selection>

Alex Lee organizes the course by the current IB topic codes, so begin with the explanation that includes both the core and HL boundaries.

**Watch for:**

- Where variation comes from before selection begins.
- Why fitness means relative reproductive success rather than strength or survival alone.
- Why individuals are selected but allele frequencies change in populations.
- Which content begins at HL: gene pools, selection patterns, Hardy–Weinberg and artificial selection.

**Pause at:** the first full explanation of natural selection. Write the chain from variation to changed allele frequency without copying the screen, then say where heredity enters the chain.

## If that didn’t land

**Amoeba Sisters — natural selection**

- Channel: <https://www.youtube.com/@AmoebaSisters> — verified
- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Amoeba+Sisters+natural+selection>

Choose this if the main mechanism still feels abstract. It gives a more visual explanation, but return to the IB material for gene pools and Hardy–Weinberg.

**Khan Academy — natural selection and population genetics**

- Channel: <https://www.youtube.com/@khanacademy> — verified
- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Khan+Academy+natural+selection+population+genetics+Hardy+Weinberg>

Choose this instead if the biology makes sense but the allele-frequency calculations don't. Stop after one worked calculation and reproduce it on paper with different numbers.

## Worth it once, not for revision

This topic doesn't contain a spatial or mechanical process that needs a second animation. Use that time to sketch three selection graphs and work a Hardy–Weinberg calculation from a blank page.
