# D2.1 Cell and nuclear division

- **Theme:** D — Continuity and change
- **Level:** Cells
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Cells copy and separate chromosomes under controlled conditions, using mitosis for continuity and meiosis for haploid, genetically varied nuclei.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

D2.1 is hard because several diagrams look alike while showing different things: homologous chromosomes pairing, sister chromatids separating and whole cells dividing. Don’t study the diagrams as pictures. For every stage, say what structure is moving, where it’s moving and what happens to chromosome number. That same language earns marks in reproduction, inheritance and cancer questions.

## In your study guide

Allott, _Biology Study Guide_ (2023), **section D2.1, pp. 94–98**. Read it *after*
the capture sheet, never before. It's condensed enough to make a fast check of
what you left out, and a slow way to meet the topic for the first time.
