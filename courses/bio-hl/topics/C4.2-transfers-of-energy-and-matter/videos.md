# C4.2 Transfers of energy and matter — what to watch

Watch **one** main video, then close it and draw the pathways from memory. A second explanation of the same material builds recognition, and recognition is the problem you’re trying to leave behind.

## Main

**Alex Lee Biology — C4.2 Transfers of energy and matter**

- Channel: <https://www.youtube.com/@misterleescience> — verified
- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Mister+Lee+Science+C4.2+Transfers+of+energy+and+matter>

Alex Lee organizes the course by the current IB topic codes, so start with the explanation built for C4.2.

**Watch for:**

- Which way arrows point in a food chain and what passes along them.
- Why energy becomes unavailable to the next trophic level even though energy hasn’t been destroyed.
- How primary production differs from secondary production.
- Why carbon cycles but energy flows in one direction.

**Pause at:** the first complete energy pyramid. Cover its labels, identify every trophic level, state the units and calculate one percentage transfer using adjacent levels.

- Channel: <https://www.youtube.com/@AmoebaSisters> — verified

Use this for a more visual explanation of feeding relationships. Afterward, draw a food web and explain each arrow without replaying the video.

## Worth it once, not for revision

A food web has several pathways operating at once, so an **animation** earns one exception to the one-video rule. Movement can make the directions of energy transfer and carbon transfer easier to track.

- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=ecosystem+energy+flow+carbon+cycle+animation>

After watching, draw the same system twice. On the first drawing, trace energy from its external source until it leaves as heat. On the second, trace one carbon atom until it returns to the environment.
