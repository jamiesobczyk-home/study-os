# C2.2 Neural signalling

- **Theme:** C — Interaction and interdependence
- **Level:** Cells
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Neurons use ion movements to generate electrical signals, carry them along axons and pass information across synapses to other cells.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

C2.2 is difficult because the same ions appear in different places with different jobs: Na+ and K+ shape the membrane potential, Ca2+ triggers neurotransmitter release, and ions entering the postsynaptic cell determine whether it reaches threshold. Learn it as a sequence of membrane events, then redraw the action-potential graph and synapse from memory. That gives you a route through questions on signalling, pain, nervous-system integration and the effects of drugs.
