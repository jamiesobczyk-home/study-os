# D2.2 Gene expression

- **Theme:** D — Continuity and change
- **Level:** Cells
- **Level of study:** HL only
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Cells control when genes are transcribed, how long their mRNA lasts and which proteins are produced, allowing one genome to generate different cell types and respond to the environment.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

D2.2 links several layers that are easy to recognize separately and hard to produce as one explanation: regulatory DNA, transcription factors, mRNA lifetime, epigenetic tags, proteins and phenotype. Practice the direction of each chain. Say what changes, what stays unchanged and how the next step follows. That gives you a structure for questions about differentiation, hormones, pollution, twins and genomic imprinting.

## In your study guide

the capture sheet, never before. It's condensed enough to make a fast check of
what you left out, and a slow way to meet the topic for the first time.
