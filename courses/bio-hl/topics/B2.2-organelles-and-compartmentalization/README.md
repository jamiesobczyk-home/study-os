# B2.2 Organelles and compartmentalization

- **Theme:** B — Form and function
- **Level:** Cells
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Cells use specialized organelles and membrane-bound compartments to concentrate reactions, separate incompatible processes and move proteins through organized pathways.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

B2.2 connects cell diagrams to explanations. The hard part isn’t recognizing a mitochondrion or Golgi apparatus; it’s producing the chain from structure to compartment to function. Learn each named space and membrane by asking what happens there, what it’s separated from and why that separation helps. Those explanations return in respiration, photosynthesis, protein synthesis and cell division.

## In your study guide

the capture sheet, never before. It's condensed enough to make a fast check of
