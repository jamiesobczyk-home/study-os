# C1.3 Photosynthesis

- **Theme:** C — Interaction and interdependence
- **Level:** Molecules
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Photosynthesis captures light energy, uses water and carbon dioxide to make carbon compounds, and links electron transfer in thylakoids to carbon fixation in the Calvin cycle.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

C1.3 gets difficult when diagrams turn into arrows you recognize but can’t explain. Learn each arrow as a transfer: what moves, where it moves, and what provides the energy. That approach handles spectra and limiting factors at SL, then keeps electrons, protons, ATP, reduced NADP and carbon compounds from getting mixed up at HL.

## In your study guide

Allott, _Biology Study Guide_ (2023), **section C1.3, pp. 32–37**. Read it *after*
the capture sheet, never before. It's condensed enough to make a fast check of
what you left out, and a slow way to meet the topic for the first time.
