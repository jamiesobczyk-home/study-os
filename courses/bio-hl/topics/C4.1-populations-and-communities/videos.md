# C4.1 Populations and communities — what to watch

Watch **one** main video, then close it and produce the population-growth and sampling explanations yourself. A second explanation usually strengthens recognition, which is the problem you’re trying to expose.

## Main

**Mister Lee Science — C4.1 Populations and communities**

- Channel: <https://www.youtube.com/@misterleescience> — verified
- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Mister+Lee+Science+C4.1+Populations+and+communities>

Alex Lee organizes the course by the current IB topic codes, so begin with the explanation built for C4.1.

**Watch for:**

- Which sampling method fits a sessile organism and which fits a motile organism.
- Why density-dependent factors push a population back toward carrying capacity.
- How exponential growth changes into the slower phase of sigmoid growth.
- What distinguishes top-down control from bottom-up control.

**Pause at:** the first population-growth graph. Cover its labels, identify exponential growth and carrying capacity, then explain aloud why the growth rate changes as density rises.

## Worth it once, not for revision

Predator and prey populations change with a time delay, so an **animation** earns one extra watch. It can make the offset between the two peaks clearer than a still graph.

- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=predator+prey+population+cycle+animation+snowshoe+hare+lynx>

Pause when prey numbers begin to fall. Predict what happens next to predator numbers and explain the delay before allowing the animation to continue.
