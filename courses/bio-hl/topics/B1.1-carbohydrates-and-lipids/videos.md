# B1.1 Carbohydrates and lipids — what to watch

Watch **one** main video, then close it and draw or explain the structures from memory. A second explanation of the same material builds recognition, which is already the skill carrying too much of the work.

## Main

**Mister Lee Science — B1.1 Carbohydrates and lipids**

- Channel: <https://www.youtube.com/@misterleescience> — verified
- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Mister+Lee+Science+B1.1+Carbohydrates+and+lipids>

Alex Lee organizes the course by the current IB topic codes, so begin with the explanation designed for B1.1.

**Watch for:**

- How condensation and hydrolysis change bonds and water molecules.
- Why starch, glycogen and cellulose behave differently even though all three contain glucose.
- How fatty-acid saturation changes shape, packing and melting point.
- Why phospholipids form a bilayer without anyone arranging them.

**Pause at:** the first comparison of starch, glycogen and cellulose. Draw all three from memory, label alpha- or beta-glucose, and add one structure-to-function link for each.

## Worth it once, not for revision

Bilayer formation earns an **animation** because the orientation of moving phospholipids is easier to understand in motion than in a finished diagram.

- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=phospholipid+bilayer+self+assembly+animation>

After watching, draw phospholipids scattered in water and then arranged in a bilayer. Label which regions interact with water and which become hidden from it.
