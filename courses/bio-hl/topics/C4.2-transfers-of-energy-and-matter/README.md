# C4.2 Transfers of energy and matter

- **Theme:** C — Interaction and interdependence
- **Level:** Ecosystems
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Energy enters ecosystems, flows through food chains and leaves as heat, while carbon and other chemical elements are transferred and recycled.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

C4.2 combines diagrams, definitions, calculations and graph analysis, so recognizing a food web won’t be enough. Practice producing two separate stories: trace energy in one direction from its source to heat, then trace matter around a cycle. That separation prevents most of the topic’s common errors and connects directly to respiration, photosynthesis and climate change.

## In your study guide

Allott, _Biology Study Guide_ (2023), **section C4.2, pp. 208–213**. Read it *after*
the capture sheet, never before. It's condensed enough to make a fast check of
what you left out, and a slow way to meet the topic for the first time.
