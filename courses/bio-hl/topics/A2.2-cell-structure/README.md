# A2.2 Cell structure

- **Theme:** A — Unity and diversity
- **Level:** Cells
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

A2.2 is about recognizing what cells share, explaining how their structures differ, using microscopes to investigate them and tracing how specialized and complex cells evolved.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

This topic combines diagrams, microscope calculations, organelle functions and evolutionary explanations, so recognizing a labeled cell won’t carry the whole question. Practice producing comparisons from a blank page and defending every micrograph identification with visible evidence. Those skills return in membranes, organelles, specialization, respiration, photosynthesis and gene expression.

## In your study guide

Allott, _Biology Study Guide_ (2023), **section A2.2, pp. 59–66**. Read it *after*
the capture sheet, never before. It's condensed enough to make a fast check of
what you left out, and a slow way to meet the topic for the first time.
