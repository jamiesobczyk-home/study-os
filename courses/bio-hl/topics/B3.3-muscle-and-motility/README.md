# B3.3 Muscle and motility

- **Theme:** B — Form and function
- **Level:** Organisms
- **Level of study:** HL only
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Muscles use ATP-powered sliding of actin and myosin to pull on skeletons, move joints and produce the controlled movements animals need.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

B3.3 runs through several scales at once: proteins slide, sarcomeres shorten, muscle fibres contract, tendons pull bones and joints move. That jump between scales is what makes the topic hard. Practice producing the whole chain, and keep a separate picture in your head for relaxation, where antagonistic muscles and elastic titin matter.

## In your study guide

Allott, _Biology Study Guide_ (2023), **section B3.3, pp. 129–132**. Read it *after*
the capture sheet, never before. It's condensed enough to make a fast check of
what you left out, and a slow way to meet the topic for the first time.
