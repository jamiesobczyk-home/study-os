# C3.1 Integration of body systems — exam practice

> Write each answer before revealing the mark scheme. A point that looked familiar after the reveal wasn’t recalled and didn’t score.

## Q1. Compare and contrast nervous and hormonal signaling between organs. **[4]**

### Mark scheme

- Nervous signaling uses electrical impulses along neurons and neurotransmitters at synapses, whereas hormonal signaling uses chemical messengers carried in blood. **[1]**
- Nervous responses are usually faster than hormonal responses. **[1]**
- Nervous responses are usually shorter-lived than hormonal responses. **[1]**
- Nervous signals are directed to connected effectors, whereas hormones may circulate widely but affect only target cells with appropriate receptors. **[1]**

_Examiner note: “nerves are fast and hormones are slow” earns one comparison. Full marks require comparison of the signal, duration and targeting as well._

## Q2. Explain the pathway of a pain withdrawal reflex when a finger touches a hot surface. **[5]**

### Mark scheme

- A free sensory nerve ending / pain receptor in the finger detects the harmful stimulus. **[1]**
- A sensory neuron carries impulses to the spinal cord. **[1]**
- A single interneuron in the grey matter of the spinal cord transfers the signal between neurons. **[1]**
- A motor neuron carries impulses from the spinal cord to a skeletal muscle. **[1]**
- The skeletal muscle contracts, withdrawing the finger in a rapid involuntary response. **[1]**

_Examiner note: an answer that jumps from sensory neuron to motor neuron misses the interneuron specified for this reflex arc. The skeletal muscle must also be identified as the effector._

## Q3. Explain feedback control of ventilation rate during vigorous exercise. **[5]**

### Mark scheme

- Increased cellular respiration during exercise produces more carbon dioxide. **[1]**
- Carbon dioxide in blood causes an increase in hydrogen ion concentration / a decrease in blood pH. **[1]**
- Chemoreceptors in the brainstem / carotid or aortic bodies detect the change in blood chemistry. **[1]**
- The medulla coordinates the response and sends increased nerve impulses to the diaphragm and intercostal muscles. **[1]**
- Faster / deeper contraction of respiratory muscles increases ventilation and removes carbon dioxide more rapidly. **[1]**

_Examiner note: “muscles need more oxygen, so breathing increases” doesn’t give the feedback pathway. The scoring chain runs through carbon dioxide, pH, chemoreceptors and the medulla._

## Q4. Explain how epinephrine coordinates the body’s response to vigorous activity. **[4]**

### Mark scheme

Award any four:

- Epinephrine is secreted by the adrenal glands and transported in blood. **[1]**
- Heart rate / force of heart contraction / cardiac output increases. **[1]**
- Bronchi and bronchioles dilate / ventilation increases. **[1]**
- Glycogen breakdown / glucose release increases, making more respiratory substrate available. **[1]**
- Blood flow is increased toward skeletal muscles. **[1]**
- The combined effects increase delivery of oxygen and glucose for intense muscle contraction. **[1]**

_Examiner note: listing “fight-or-flight” doesn’t identify a physiological effect. Each mark needs a named change, and the final point requires linking the changes to muscle respiration._

## Q5. Explain positive phototropism in a plant shoot exposed to lateral light. **[5]**

### Mark scheme

- Auxin becomes more concentrated on the shaded side of the shoot. **[1]**
- Auxin promotes active secretion of hydrogen ions into the apoplast / cell wall. **[1]**
- Lower pH loosens cross-links between cellulose components in the cell wall. **[1]**
- Cells on the shaded side elongate more than cells on the illuminated side. **[1]**
- Unequal elongation causes the shoot to curve toward the light. **[1]**

_Examiner note: saying that auxin moves the shoot toward light treats bending as movement of the whole plant. Full marks require unequal cell elongation caused by an auxin gradient._

## Q6. Explain how positive feedback involving ethylene benefits a ripening fruit. **[4]**

### Mark scheme

- Ethylene / ethene stimulates changes associated with fruit ripening. **[1]**
- Ripening stimulates increased ethylene production. **[1]**
- Increased ethylene causes further ripening / the response is amplified in the same direction. **[1]**
- Accept: the feedback produces rapid and synchronized ripening, aiding coordinated seed dispersal / attraction of animals when seeds are ready. **[1]**

_Examiner note: naming ethylene and ripening scores at most one mark. Positive feedback requires both directions of the loop and the amplification that follows._
