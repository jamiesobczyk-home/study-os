# D2.3 Water potential

- **Theme:** D — Continuity and change
- **Level:** Cells
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Water moves across partially permeable membranes according to differences in solute concentration and water potential, producing different effects in cells with and without walls.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

D2.3 uses several pairs of terms that sound interchangeable but aren't: movement and net movement, hypotonic and low water potential, solute potential and total water potential. The calculations are short; choosing the right values and explaining the biological result is harder. Practice every prediction as a chain: compare the two sides, identify the direction of net water movement, then name the effect on the cell.
