# D3.3 Homeostasis

- **Theme:** D — Continuity and change
- **Level:** Organisms
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Homeostasis uses negative feedback to keep internal conditions within limits, with hormones, nerves and organs coordinating control of glucose, temperature, water balance and blood supply.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

D3.3 contains several feedback loops that look alike until the names start piling up. Learn each one as the same sequence: regulated variable, receptor, control center, signal, effector and response. Then attach the correct cells, hormones and organs. At HL, keep the kidney processes in their locations: ultrafiltration at the renal corpuscle, selective reabsorption mainly in the proximal convoluted tubule, the medullary gradient from the loop of Henle and ADH-controlled water reabsorption in the collecting duct.
