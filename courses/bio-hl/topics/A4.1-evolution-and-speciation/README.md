# A4.1 Evolution and speciation

- **Theme:** A — Unity and diversity
- **Level:** Ecosystems
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Evolution changes heritable characteristics in populations, while reproductive isolation and different selection pressures can split one ancestral species into several new ones.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

A4.1 asks you to move between evidence and process. Sequence data, selective breeding and homologous structures show that evolution has occurred, while isolation and differential selection explain how species form. The hard part is producing the causal chain instead of recognizing its vocabulary. Practice saying what stops gene flow, what changes next and how that change ends in reproductive isolation.

## In your study guide
