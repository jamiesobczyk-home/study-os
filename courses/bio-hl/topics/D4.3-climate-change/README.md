# D4.3 Climate change

- **Theme:** D — Continuity and change
- **Level:** Ecosystems
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Human-caused increases in carbon dioxide and methane drive warming that can amplify itself, disrupt ecosystems and alter natural selection.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

D4.3 tests causal chains more than isolated facts. You need to move from a driver to a mechanism and then to a biological consequence: warming to ice loss to lower albedo, or surface warming to reduced upwelling to lower primary production. Draw each process as arrows, close every feedback loop, and keep acidification separate from bleaching.

## In your study guide

Allott, _Biology Study Guide_ (2023), **section D4.3, pp. 227–232**. Read it *after*
the capture sheet, never before. It's condensed enough to make a fast check of
what you left out, and a slow way to meet the topic for the first time.
