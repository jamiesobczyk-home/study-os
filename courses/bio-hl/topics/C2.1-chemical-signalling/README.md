# C2.1 Chemical signalling

- **Theme:** C — Interaction and interdependence
- **Level:** Cells
- **Level of study:** HL only
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Cells detect specific chemical ligands with receptors, then convert that binding event into responses ranging from ion movement and enzyme activation to glucose uptake and altered gene expression.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

C2.1 is difficult because several pathways begin with the same event, ligand binding, and then diverge. Memorizing the pathway names won’t reliably separate them. Practice drawing what stays outside, what crosses the membrane, where the receptor sits and what changes at the end. That framework also supports neural signalling, reproduction, gene expression and homeostasis.

## In your study guide

the capture sheet, never before. It's condensed enough to make a fast check of
what you left out, and a slow way to meet the topic for the first time.
