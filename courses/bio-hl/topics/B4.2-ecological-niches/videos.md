# B4.2 Ecological niches — what to watch

Watch **one** main video, then close it and produce the topic from memory. A second explanation of the same material builds recognition, which is the skill that has been covering up the gaps.

## Main

**Alex Lee Biology — B4.2 Ecological niches**

- Channel: <https://www.youtube.com/@misterleescience> — verified
- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Mister+Lee+Science+B4.2+Ecological+niches>

Alex Lee organizes the course by the current IB topic codes, so start with the explanation built for B4.2.

**Watch for:**

- What belongs in an ecological niche besides the organism’s habitat.
- How the five nutritional patterns differ: photosynthetic, holozoic, mixotrophic, saprotrophic and the diverse nutrition of archaea.
- How adaptations for feeding, defense and light harvesting affect a species’ role.
- Why competition makes a realized niche differ from a fundamental niche.

**Pause at:** the first comparison of fundamental and realized niches. Draw two overlapping ranges, label the larger potential range and the smaller actual range, then explain aloud what caused the restriction.

## Worth it once, not for revision

Hominid diet questions may give you a skull or teeth you haven’t seen before. A rotating **3D skull comparison** is worth watching once because it shows tooth surfaces, jaw size and muscle attachment areas from angles that a flat diagram hides.

- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=hominid+dentition+diet+3D+skull+comparison>

Pause on two contrasting skulls. Describe the incisors, canines, premolars, molars and jaw before you infer diet. Evidence comes before the conclusion.
