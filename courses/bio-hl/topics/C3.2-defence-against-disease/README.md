# C3.2 Defence against disease

- **Theme:** C — Interaction and interdependence
- **Level:** Organisms
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

The body blocks pathogens, attacks those that enter, remembers their antigens and uses vaccination and herd immunity to reduce disease in individuals and populations.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

C3.2 is a chain of events, but it’s often studied as a pile of cell names. Learn who recognizes what, what activates what, and what each cell becomes. Then keep the scales separate: clotting and phagocytosis act locally, immune memory protects an individual, and herd immunity changes transmission across a population. Producing those chains will earn more marks than recognizing the diagram after it appears.
