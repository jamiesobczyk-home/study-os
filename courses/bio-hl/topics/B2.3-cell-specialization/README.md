# B2.3 Cell specialization

- **Theme:** B — Form and function
- **Level:** Cells
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Cells with the same genome become different specialists by expressing different genes, then develop sizes and structures fitted to their particular functions.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

B2.3 combines three kinds of answer that are easy to recognize and harder to produce: developmental chains, surface area-to-volume reasoning and structure-to-function comparisons. Practice saying the link in full. Name the structure, state what it changes, and connect that change to the cell's function. That pattern carries into gas exchange, kidney transport, muscle and reproduction.

## In your study guide

Allott, _Biology Study Guide_ (2023), **section B2.3, pp. 77–79**. Read it *after*
the capture sheet, never before. It's condensed enough to make a fast check of
what you left out, and a slow way to meet the topic for the first time.
