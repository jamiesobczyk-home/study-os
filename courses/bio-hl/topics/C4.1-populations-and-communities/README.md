# C4.1 Populations and communities

- **Theme:** C — Interaction and interdependence
- **Level:** Ecosystems
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Population size changes through sampling, resource limits and interactions within communities, from competition and cooperation to predation, mutualism and chemical interference.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

C4.1 mixes definitions, calculations, graphs, experimental design and ecological explanations. That makes recognition especially risky: a familiar sigmoid curve won’t help if you can’t explain why it bends. Practice producing the chain from rising density to stronger limiting factors to lower population growth, and keep association separate from proof when interpreting field data.

## In your study guide

Allott, _Biology Study Guide_ (2023), **section C4.1, pp. 200–207**. Read it *after*
the capture sheet, never before. It’s condensed enough to make a fast check of
what you left out, and a slow way to meet the topic for the first time.
