# D1.3 Mutation and gene editing

- **Theme:** D — Continuity and change
- **Level:** Molecules
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Mutations create new DNA variation, while knockout experiments and CRISPR-Cas9 let scientists investigate or deliberately alter selected genes.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

D1.3 tests whether you can follow a change across levels: DNA sequence, codons, amino acids, protein function, phenotype and inheritance. The hard part isn’t recognizing the mutation names. It’s producing the whole causal chain and keeping mutation, selection and gene editing separate. Practice on short sequences with the explanation covered, then say what changes and what might stay unchanged.
