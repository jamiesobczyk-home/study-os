# C1.3 Photosynthesis — what to watch

Watch **one** main video, then close it and draw the process from memory. A second explanation of the same pathway builds recognition, and recognition is the problem you’re trying to replace with recall.

## Main

**Mister Lee Science — C1.3 Photosynthesis**

- Channel: <https://www.youtube.com/@misterleescience> — verified
- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Mister+Lee+Science+C1.3+Photosynthesis>

Alex Lee organizes the course by the current IB topic codes, so begin with the explanation designed around C1.3.

**Watch for:**

- Which substances supply the carbon, hydrogen and released oxygen in photosynthesis.
- How an absorption spectrum differs from an action spectrum.
- Where electrons, protons, ATP and reduced NADP move during the light-dependent reactions.
- What happens to RuBP, glycerate 3-phosphate and triose phosphate in the Calvin cycle.

**Pause at:** the first complete diagram linking the light-dependent reactions to the Calvin cycle. Cover its labels and rebuild it with two boxes. Your arrows must show water and light entering the first box, carbon dioxide entering the second, ATP and reduced NADP moving to the second, and ADP, phosphate and NADP returning.

## Worth it once, not for revision

Chemiosmosis is spatial: the side of the thylakoid membrane matters, and ATP synthase only works because protons return through it. An **animation** earns one extra watch here.

- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=photosynthesis+thylakoid+chemiosmosis+animation>

After watching, draw a thylakoid membrane with the stroma above it and the lumen below it. Add the direction of proton pumping, proton flow through ATP synthase and ATP production. If your diagram has no named compartments, redraw it before moving on.
