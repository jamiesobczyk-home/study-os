# B3.1 Gas exchange

- **Theme:** B — Form and function
- **Level:** Organisms
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Gas exchange depends on thin, moist surfaces, steep diffusion gradients and ventilation or transport, with lungs, leaves and haemoglobin adapted to keep gases moving where they’re needed.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

B3.1 combines anatomy, pressure changes, diffusion, plant water loss and graph interpretation. The hard part isn’t recognizing an alveolus or a leaf. It’s producing the causal chain from structure to diffusion and from muscle movement to pressure. Practice those chains aloud, then draw the ventilation and dissociation-curve diagrams from memory.
