# C2.1 Chemical signalling — what to watch

Watch **one** main video, then close it and draw the pathways from memory. A second explanation can feel productive while you’re still recognizing the same diagrams, which is the problem this pack is meant to expose.

## Main

**Mister Lee Science — C2.1 Chemical signalling**

- Channel: <https://www.youtube.com/@misterleescience> — verified
- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Mister+Lee+Science+C2.1+Chemical+signalling>

Alex Lee organizes the course by the current IB topic codes, so begin with the explanation made for C2.1.

**Watch for:**

- Why a hormone can reach many cells in the blood but affect only its target cells.
- How ligand properties determine whether the receptor is in the membrane or inside the cell.
- What crosses the membrane in each pathway: ions, the ligand, neither, or a glucose transporter vesicle.
- How the acetylcholine, epinephrine, insulin and steroid pathways end in different cellular responses.

**Pause at:** the first comparison of transmembrane and intracellular receptors. Draw both cells, locate each receptor, and say aloud why a hydrophilic ligand stays outside while a steroid can enter.

## Worth it once, not for revision

Receptor activation involves changes in protein shape, opening channels and activating proteins on opposite sides of a membrane. An **animation** earns an extra watch because those movements are hard to read from a static pathway diagram.

- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=GPCR+G+protein+cAMP+animation>
- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=insulin+receptor+GLUT4+translocation+animation>

After watching, draw each pathway as numbered steps. Put epinephrine, its receptor, the G protein and cAMP in different positions. For insulin, make the glucose transporter move to the membrane rather than making insulin carry glucose.
