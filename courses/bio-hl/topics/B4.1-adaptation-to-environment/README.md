# B4.1 Adaptation to environment

- **Theme:** B — Form and function
- **Level:** Ecosystems
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Environmental conditions filter where species can live, while natural selection produces adaptations that link each organism’s form to its habitat.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

B4.1 combines named examples, graphs and causal explanations, so recognizing a picture of a cactus won’t carry an exam answer. Practice every example as a three-part chain: environmental problem, named feature, biological advantage. The same chain then explains tolerance ranges, distributions, biomes and convergent evolution.

## In your study guide

Allott, _Biology Study Guide_ (2023), **section B4.1, pp. 190–194**. Read it *after*
the capture sheet, never before. It's condensed enough to make a fast check of
what you left out, and a slow way to meet the topic for the first time.
