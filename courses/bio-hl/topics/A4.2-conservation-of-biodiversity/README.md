# A4.2 Conservation of biodiversity

- **Theme:** A — Unity and diversity
- **Level:** Ecosystems
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Conservation biology measures biodiversity loss, traces its human causes and combines habitat protection, restoration, managed populations and stored genetic material to reduce further loss.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

A4.2 can feel like a list of threats and conservation methods, but most exam marks come from links: a human action causes a biological pressure, surveys provide evidence of change, and a species’ needs determine the conservation response. Learn the named case studies as causal chains, then practice choosing evidence and conservation methods for unfamiliar situations. There’s no additional HL-only content, so the same precise explanations matter at both levels.
