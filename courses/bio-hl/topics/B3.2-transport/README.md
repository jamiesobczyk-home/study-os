# B3.2 Transport

- **Theme:** B — Form and function
- **Level:** Organisms
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Transport systems use specialized vessels, pressure differences and exchange surfaces to move blood, water and dissolved substances through animals and plants.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

B3.2 is difficult because several mechanisms use the same words, especially pressure, flow, diffusion and active transport, while meaning different things. Learn each as a causal sequence: what creates the pressure difference, what moves, where it moves and which structure makes that possible. That turns vessel diagrams and cardiac-cycle pictures into explanations you can produce for marks.

## In your study guide

Allott, _Biology Study Guide_ (2023), **section B3.2, pp. 121–128**. Read it *after*
the capture sheet, never before. It's condensed enough to make a fast check of
what you left out, and a slow way to meet the topic for the first time.
