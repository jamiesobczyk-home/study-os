# B4.2 Ecological niches

- **Theme:** B — Form and function
- **Level:** Ecosystems
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

A species’ niche combines how it gets matter and energy, the adaptations it uses, the conditions it tolerates and the interactions that determine the role it actually occupies.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

B4.2 asks you to move between definitions, unfamiliar organisms and cause-and-effect explanations. The hard part isn’t recognizing an adaptation. It’s explaining what the feature does, how that changes survival or feeding, and how interactions narrow the niche a species actually occupies. Practice producing those links aloud, especially for skull evidence and competition scenarios.

## In your study guide

the capture sheet, never before. It's condensed enough to make a fast check of
