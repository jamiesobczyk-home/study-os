# C1.1 Enzymes and metabolism

- **Theme:** C — Interaction and interdependence
- **Level:** Molecules
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Enzymes use specific, changeable active sites to lower activation energy, control metabolic pathways and regulate how quickly cells build, break down and transform molecules.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

C1.1 connects protein shape, molecular collisions, energy and whole metabolic pathways. The hard part is keeping several similar explanations separate: low temperature reduces collisions, high temperature can denature, extra substrate can overcome competitive inhibition, and it can’t restore the maximum rate during non-competitive inhibition. Draw each mechanism, cover the labels and explain the causal chain aloud.
