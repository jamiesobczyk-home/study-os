# D3.1 Reproduction — what to watch

Watch **one** main video, then close it and reconstruct the processes from memory. A second explanation of the same material builds recognition, which is already the problem. Use another video only when you can name the part you still can’t produce.

## Main

**Alex Lee Biology — D3.1 Reproduction**

- Channel: <https://www.youtube.com/@misterleescience> — verified
- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Mister+Lee+Science+D3.1+Reproduction>

Alex Lee organizes the course by the current IB topic codes, so start with the explanation built for D3.1.

**Watch for:**

- How meiosis, fertilization and mitosis occupy different places in a sexual life cycle.
- Which hormone causes each menstrual-cycle event, and which organ produces that hormone.
- How pollination, fertilization, seed dispersal and germination differ.
- Where the HL sequence begins with GnRH and ends with pregnancy, childbirth and evidence about HRT.

**Pause at:** the first menstrual-cycle graph. Cover the labels and explain the sequence from FSH-driven follicle development to menstruation. Include the LH surge, ovulation, the corpus luteum and the fall in progesterone.

## Worth it once, not for revision

Pollen-tube growth and human fertilization involve structures moving and changing, so an **animation** earns one exception to the one-video rule.

- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=flowering+plant+pollination+pollen+tube+fertilization+animation>
- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=human+fertilization+acrosome+cortical+reaction+animation>

After watching, draw one sequence from memory. Label where the gametes are, when their membranes interact and what prevents additional sperm from entering the human egg.
