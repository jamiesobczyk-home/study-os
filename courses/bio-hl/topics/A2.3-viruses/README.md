# A2.3 Viruses

- **Theme:** A — Unity and diversity
- **Level:** Cells
- **Level of study:** HL only
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Viruses are acellular genetic parasites that use host cells to reproduce, follow lytic or lysogenic routes, may have several origins and can evolve fast enough to escape immunity or treatment.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

A2.3 combines structures, two easily confused replication cycles and evolution in one short topic. The hard part is keeping the causal sequence straight. Draw where the viral genome is at each stage, then explain how mutation creates variants and selection changes their frequencies. That turns the diagrams and disease examples into answers you can produce.

## In your study guide

the capture sheet, never before. It’s condensed enough to make a fast check of
what you left out, and a slow way to meet the topic for the first time.
