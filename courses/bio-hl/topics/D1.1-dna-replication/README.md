# D1.1 DNA replication

- **Theme:** D — Continuity and change
- **Level:** Molecules
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

DNA replication uses complementary templates to preserve genetic information, while PCR and gel electrophoresis let scientists copy and analyze selected DNA outside cells.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

D1.1 combines three different stories: replication in cells, PCR in a machine and DNA separation in a gel. They use related ideas, but they aren't the same process. Learn each as an ordered sequence and make yourself name what every enzyme, temperature change or electric field actually does. At higher level, draw 5′ and 3′ labels before deciding which strand is leading or lagging.
