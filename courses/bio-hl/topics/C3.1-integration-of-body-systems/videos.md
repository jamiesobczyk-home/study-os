# C3.1 Integration of body systems — what to watch

Watch **one** main video, then close it and draw the pathways from memory. A second explanation of the same material builds recognition, and recognition is the problem you’re trying to get past.

## Main

**Mister Lee Science — C3.1 Integration of body systems**

- Channel: <https://www.youtube.com/@misterleescience> — verified
- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Mister+Lee+Science+C3.1+Integration+of+body+systems>

Alex Lee organizes the course by the current IB topic codes, so begin with the version built around C3.1.

**Watch for:**

- Where sensory input enters, where information is integrated and where motor output goes.
- How a fast neural response differs from a slower hormonal response.
- Which receptors provide feedback about blood pressure and blood chemistry.
- How the HL plant examples use signaling chemicals to coordinate growth.

**Pause at:** the first complete feedback loop. Copy only the labels, cover the arrows, and reconstruct the direction of information from receptor to integration center to effector.

## Worth it once, not for revision

A pain reflex is a spatial pathway, so an **animation** earns an extra watch. Seeing the impulse enter the spinal cord, cross through an interneuron and leave through a motor neuron can fix the direction of the pathway.

- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=pain+withdrawal+reflex+arc+animation+sensory+interneuron+motor>

After watching, draw the receptor, sensory neuron, spinal cord grey matter, interneuron, motor neuron and skeletal muscle. Add arrows before checking the animation again.
