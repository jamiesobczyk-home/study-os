# D3.1 Reproduction

- **Theme:** D — Continuity and change
- **Level:** Organisms
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Reproduction preserves a species while meiosis, fertilization, reproductive structures and hormone-controlled development determine how genetic material reaches the next generation.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

D3.1 combines several sequences that are easy to recognize and hard to produce: the menstrual cycle, flowering-plant reproduction, gametogenesis, fertilization and pregnancy. Learn each as a causal chain rather than a labeled list. Cover the diagram or graph, name the event that changes first, and explain what causes the next event before checking your notes.

## In your study guide

Allott, _Biology Study Guide_ (2023), **section D3.1, pp. 151–160**. Read it *after*
the capture sheet, never before. It's condensed enough to make a fast check of
what you left out, and a slow way to meet the topic for the first time.
