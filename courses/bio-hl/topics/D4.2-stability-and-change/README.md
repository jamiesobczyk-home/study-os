# D4.2 Stability and change

- **Theme:** D — Continuity and change
- **Level:** Ecosystems
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

This topic explains what keeps ecosystems functioning, how human pressures can destabilize them, and how communities change through succession or restoration.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

D4.2 is difficult because its answers are chains, not labels. “Eutrophication,” “tipping point” and “rewilding” earn few marks unless you can produce the steps connecting a cause to a change in ecosystem processes. Learn each case as arrows between events, then practice rebuilding the arrows without looking. The textbook heading “Sustainability and changes” refers to this topic, but **Stability and change** is the syllabus title.

## In your study guide

Allott, _Biology Study Guide_ (2023), **section D4.2, pp. 220–226**. Read it *after*
the capture sheet, never before. It’s condensed enough to make a fast check of
what you left out, and a slow way to meet the topic for the first time.
