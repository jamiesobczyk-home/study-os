# B4.1 Adaptation to environment — what to watch

Watch **one** main video, then close it and produce a tolerance curve, a biome comparison and two named adaptations from memory. A second explanation can build recognition again, which is the problem you’re trying to get past.

## Main

**Alex Lee Biology — B4.1 Adaptation to environment**

- Channel: <https://www.youtube.com/@misterleescience> — verified
- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Mister+Lee+Science+B4.1+Adaptation+to+environment>

Alex Lee organizes the course by the current IB topic codes, so start with the explanation built around B4.1.

**Watch for:**

- How an adaptation changes the range of abiotic conditions a species can tolerate.
- Why a correlation along a transect can support an explanation of distribution but can’t prove causation.
- How temperature and rainfall predict terrestrial biomes.
- Which named feature solves which environmental problem in dunes, mangroves, deserts and rainforests.

**Pause at:** the first tolerance or distribution graph. Cover the labels, identify the optimum, stress zones and tolerance limits, then explain what would happen to abundance as the variable moved away from the optimum.

## Worth it once, not for revision

A biome diagram places ecosystems in a two-dimensional space, with temperature on one axis and rainfall on the other. Seeing a guided plot once can make that spatial relationship clearer than memorizing six separate descriptions.

- Search: <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Whittaker+biome+diagram+temperature+precipitation+explained>

Pause before the biome names appear. Predict where tundra, hot desert and tropical forest belong, then give the temperature-and-rainfall reason for each position.
