# B1.1 Carbohydrates and lipids

- **Theme:** B — Form and function
- **Level:** Molecules
- **Level of study:** SL and HL
- **Status:** ready

## The one-sentence version

Small changes in glucose, fatty acids and attached chemical groups produce carbohydrates and lipids with very different roles in energy storage, cell recognition, support and membranes.

## Why this topic is worth getting right

B1.1 is difficult because several molecules share the same components but behave differently. The reliable route is to produce a chain for each one: structure, chemical property, biological function. That approach separates starch from cellulose, fats from oils and triglycerides from phospholipids, and it prepares you for membranes, respiration and cell recognition.
